2026 The Open Pool

Hosted by Joe Rodriguez, Jeff Arnold, Elio Duarte & Cristobal Escudero

2026 Open Championship
Royal Birkdale Golf Club • Southport, England • July 16–19, 2026

How to Enter

  • Pick 5 players from the field.
  • Pick a 6th alternate — replaces a pick that withdraws before teeing off.
  • Add a tiebreaker: predict the winner's score to par.
  • Submit by the deadline — entries close at first tee time Thursday.
  • Enter as many teams as you like at $25 each.
  • Entering multiple teams? Use the same email and your previous picks will appear automatically for reference.

Scoring

  • Need 3 picks to make the cut to qualify.
  • The best 3 scores (lowest to par) count.
  • Entry with the lowest combined score wins.
  • 1st place ties broken by closest tiebreaker to winner's final score — only at tournament end.
  • 2nd & 3rd place ties split the prize evenly between tied entries.

Prize Pool — $25 per entry

  • 70% — 1st Place
  • 20% — 2nd Place
  • 10% — 3rd Place
